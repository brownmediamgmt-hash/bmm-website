03Statistieken zonder cookies

De site telt bezoeken op een manier die je niet volgt:

  • Er worden geen cookies geplaatst. Een willekeurige code in je browser (sessionStorage) houdt één bezoek bij elkaar en verdwijnt zodra je het tabblad sluit.
  • Je IP-adres wordt niet opgeslagen. Op de server wordt er een anonieme bezoekerscode van gemaakt die elke dag verandert, zodat bezoeken niet over meerdere dagen aan elkaar te koppelen zijn.
  • De stad of provincie wordt bepaald met een database op mijn eigen server; je IP-adres gaat daarvoor niet naar een ander bedrijf.
  • Staat in je browser “Do Not Track” of “Global Privacy Control” aan? Dan word je helemaal niet meegeteld.
  • De statistieken worden met niemand gedeeld en na 400 dagen automatisch gewist.

03Statistics without cookies

The site counts visits in a way that doesn’t follow you:

  • No cookies are set. A random code in your browser (sessionStorage) keeps one visit together and disappears when you close the tab.
  • Your IP address is not stored. The server turns it into an anonymous visitor code that changes every day, so visits can’t be linked across days.
  • The city or province is looked up in a database on my own server; your IP address is not sent to another company for that.
  • Is “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” switched on in your browser? Then you are not counted at all.
  • The statistics are not shared with anyone and are wiped automatically after 400 days.

04Cookies and storage in your browser

Advertising cookies from Meta (only with your consent)

I advertise on Instagram. If you click “Accept” in the cookie notice, the site loads the Meta pixel from Meta Platforms Ireland. It sets cookies and tells Meta which page you view and whether you sent a form or clicked the WhatsApp button, together with your IP address and browser details. That way I can see which ads work and Meta can show my ads to the right people. What you type into a form is not sent to Meta. Meta also uses this data for its own purposes; see Meta’s privacy policy.

If you click “Decline”, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the pixel is not loaded and the site works exactly the same. You can change your choice any time:

What else your browser keeps

Your browser also keeps a few things the site needs to work:

  • Your language choice (Dutch or English) and your cookie choice.
  • The visit code for the statistics (only while the tab is open).
  • For the intake: an interim copy of your answers, so nothing is lost if your connection drops. It is removed once you send the intake.

Videos from Vimeo and YouTube

Some videos play through Vimeo. Only when you play a video or move your mouse over one does your browser contact Vimeo, which then receives your IP address. I use Vimeo’s “do not track” setting so Vimeo doesn’t track your viewing. If a video is on YouTube, it only loads once you click play, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The preview images are fetched by my own server, so your browser doesn’t contact Vimeo or YouTube for those.

Instagram posts are not embedded on the site: you open them with a link on Instagram itself.

Fonts and scripts are served by this site itself, not by Google or another company.
